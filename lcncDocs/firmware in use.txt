Names with "pl" are for plasma setups
Names with 'sdi" have step/dir index pins for homing to index with step/dir hardware
Names with "pwm" typically replace some step/dir outputs with pwm/dir
Names with "pm" have period measurement hardware
Name with "os" have one-shot firmware
Names with "pkt" have PktUART firmware for things like MODBUS
